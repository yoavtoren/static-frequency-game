Static Frequency — Support

A first-person survival horror game for iPhone.

Contact

Write to yoavtoren@gmail.com. Tell us your iPhone model, iOS version, and what happened. A screenshot helps.

What you need

  • An iPhone XS or newer, with iOS 15 or later. The game is played with the phone held sideways.
  • Headphones. The game is meant to be heard: where a sound comes from is often the only warning you get.

How to play

  • Left thumb moves, right thumb looks. Run, Hide, Torch and Use are on the right.
  • Start the generators, throw the master breaker in the Control Room, and live through the charge.
  • Radios have no markings: turn the dial by ear until a voice comes through, then hold it there.
  • Look down to read your compass. There is no map: learn the grounds.
  • Do not stare at it.

Common questions

It's too dark to see anything. That is on purpose — but if your screen is dim or you are in a bright room, raise Settings › Picture › Brightness (it has a calibration page).

Flashing lights. The game has lightning and flickering, failing-picture effects. Settings › Picture › Reduce flashing softens all of them, and it is on automatically if Reduce Motion is on in your iPhone's settings.

No sound. Check the volume and the Sound settings in the game. The game plays even with the silent switch on.

The game feels slow or the phone gets hot. Settings › Picture › Quality: Auto lowers it by itself; Low is the lightest.

Continue a night. If the app was closed in the middle of a night, the main menu offers to continue it. If you die, the night starts again from the beginning.

Subtitles and language. Eleven languages, chosen on the start screen or in Settings.
