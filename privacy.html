Static Frequency — Privacy Policy

Effective 20 November 2026. Applies to the Static Frequency app for iPhone and iPad, the computer version and the web version of the game.

The short version

The game has no accounts and never asks who you are. It does not sell your data and does not track you across other companies' apps and websites: the app never asks for, or uses, your device's advertising identifier.

Three things leave your device: anonymous play data, which you can turn off (see Play data); in the app, a full-screen ad after you are caught, served by Unity, unless you have bought "No ads" (see Advertising in the app); and in the web version, ads from Google, which uses cookies for them (see Advertising in the web version). Everything else the game remembers stays on your device.

What is kept on your device

  • Your settings (look speed, brightness, volumes, subtitles and language, and the like).
  • Your progress: the notes you have filed in the case file, your best times, whether you have finished the night.
  • A night you were in the middle of when the app was closed, so it can be continued.
  • A short record of how your last nights ended, used only by the game on your device.

None of this leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Play data

To see how far players get and what goes wrong, the game sends anonymous events to PostHog, an analytics service, on servers in the European Union: that the game was opened; when a night starts and how it ends (the mode, how long it lasted, what caught you, how many notes you had filed); when a generator is repaired or a radio tuned; whether an ad was shown; whether a "No ads" purchase went through; and the game's own error messages. Each event carries the platform (iPhone/iPad, web or computer), the game's build number and its language.

The events are tied to a random identifier the game makes up and keeps on your device, never to your name, your Apple Account, an advertising identifier or anything else that says who you are. No recording of the screen, no taps or keystrokes, no location. We have PostHog discard IP addresses. Turn it off at any time in Settings › Controls › Share anonymous play data: nothing more is sent from then on. PostHog processes this data for us under its Privacy Policy.

Advertising in the app

The app for iPhone and iPad shows a full-screen ad when you leave the end screen after being caught, to start again or to go back to the menu. It never shows an ad while you play, nor after a night you survive. The ads are served by Unity (Unity LevelPlay, with Unity Ads), which receives from your device the technical information needed to choose, show and count an ad, such as the device model, operating system, language, a rough location derived from the IP address, and how you interacted with the ad. The app does not give Unity your advertising identifier and does not ask to track you, so the ads are not personalised from your activity in other apps; in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom they are not personalised at all. Unity handles this information under its Privacy Policy.

No ads: a one-time in-app purchase removes the ads for good, on every iPhone and iPad signed in to the same Apple Account (Settings › Controls › Ads, or the button on the end screen; "Restore purchase" brings it back on a new device). With it, the app does not start Unity's ad service at all. The purchase is handled by Apple; we receive no payment details, only that it was bought.

The computer version has no ads.

Permissions the app may ask for

  • Add to Photos — only when you choose to save a picture you took in Photo mode. The app never reads your photo library.
  • Motion sensors — only if you turn on tilt aiming in Settings. The readings are used on the device, for aiming, and never stored or sent anywhere.

The app also uses vibration and checks whether headphones are connected, to set its sound. Neither involves any personal data.

Game Center

The app is ready to use Apple's Game Center for features such as leaderboards and playing together. If you use them, Game Center is operated by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; we do not receive your Apple ID or any personal information from it.

The web version

The web version is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may record technical information such as IP addresses in its server logs, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. The game itself stores its settings and progress only in your browser.

Advertising in the web version

The web version (not the iPhone app) shows a full-screen ad when you leave the end screen after being caught, to start again or to go back to the menu. It never shows an ad while you play. The ads are served by Google AdSense.

Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to show and measure these ads. Where you allow it, they also use them to personalise ads based on your visits to this and other websites. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Google's consent message asks for your choice first, and you can change it later. Google handles this information under its own Privacy Policy. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services, and turn personalised ads off in My Ad Center.

We do not receive any personal information from Google: only totals, such as how many ads were shown and what they earned.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children: the play data above is anonymous. Static Frequency is a horror game and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
